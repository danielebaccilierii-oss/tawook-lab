The craft

Marinated overnight,
burnt perfectly

“Tawook is simple: yogurt, spices, patience. The charcoal does the rest. That's the whole recipe — and it takes a day.”

Every piece of chicken is cut by hand, marinated for 24 hours in yogurt and seven spices, then grilled over real charcoal — not gas, not electric. You can taste the difference in the first bite.

Our toum is whipped fresh daily. Our pickles ferment in-house. Our bread comes off the saj warm, every time.
